Check the objective separately from whether the answer is correct or whether
other answer items remain difficult. Reject if a requested answer item, even
one component of a composite task, is supplied in the question or format note.
Examples: saying "the four quarterly payment periods" while asking how many
reported periods there were; asking a change "from $10 to $12" when those source
values should come from the filing; giving the classification to be found.
Do not ban all numbers: company/report names, reporting years, dates defining
the requested scope, item enumeration, and explicitly stated operation
conventions may be legitimate. Distinguish these from the answer values or
conclusions the solver must obtain from the source. Audit word-form numbers,
paraphrases and semantic implications, not only digit matches.
