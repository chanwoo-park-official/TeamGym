Select one or more valid source QAs and at least two complete mapped blocks. The
selected QAs do not need to share one analytical purpose. When they are not
naturally related, ask a concise numbered multi-part review instead of inventing
a comparison, causal relationship, or common theme.

PIU count guidance:
- At least two evidence units are required for collaboration.
- Select the number required by the task and source evidence. Do not add QAs
  merely to increase PIU count.

Design rules:
1. Ask for a concise memo, calculation, explanation, or numbered set of answers.
2. The public question must specify what to determine without revealing
   report-specific values or expected conclusions.
3. Use only supplied source QAs and complete mapped blocks. Each selected block
   must belong to a QA in source_qa_ids_used, and each used QA must contribute at
   least one selected block. It is not necessary to use every QA or every block.
4. One normalized source block is one evidence unit. For a table containing
   unrelated rows that independently reveal another selected PIU, optionally
   specify source_row_numbers: ordered 1-based ORIGINAL table rows to retain.
   Retain the header and separator (rows 1 and 2), all rows necessary for this
   unit's claims, material units, periods, totals and scope. Python copies exact
   original rows with provenance and repeats grounding and packet checks.
   Do not restrict paragraphs or rewrite/summarize any row. Never duplicate one block
   into multiple units for separate values, rows, or claims. A single evidence
   unit may support multiple requested items and rubric claims.
5. Do not select two blocks solely for information identified as duplicated,
   contained, or derived-equivalent. Partially overlapping blocks may both be
   selected only when the public task and rubric use a unique contribution from
   each.
6. Create objectively gradable hidden claims containing the expected value,
   direction, scope, period, or conclusion.
7. Claims and evidence units have a many-to-many relationship. One claim may
   require several RIUs, and one RIU may support several claims.
8. Every selected RIU must be indispensable to at least one report-specific
   claim. Removing that RIU must leave at least one linked claim not fully
   grounded, even when all other RIUs remain.
9. Do not create artificial claims whose only purpose is to make an RIU appear
   necessary. Formatting, citation style, and generic formulas are not hidden
   report-specific claims.
10. supports_claim_ids and supporting_unit_ids must agree exactly.
11. The reference answer must satisfy every claim, show material calculations
    when applicable, preserve material units and periods, and use no outside facts.
12. Do not create answer choices, decision IDs, distractors, or plausible-option
    sets.
13. source_qa_ids_used is the subset of valid QAs materially used. It must contain
    at least one QA but need not contain every supplied QA.
14. If feedback is present, redesign the task instead of explaining the failure.
