The source blocks are immutable. Do not rewrite, concatenate, split, or invent
report text. Adjacent label/value fragments have already been normalized by the
parser.

Identify overlap among the answer-relevant information contributed by these
blocks:

- exact_duplicate: the mapped contribution is repeated
- information_contained: one block contains the entire mapped contribution of
  another block and may also contain additional useful information
- derived_equivalent: one contribution can be directly computed from another
- partial_overlap: blocks share some information but each has a unique mapped
  contribution

Exclusion rules:
1. Exclude a block only when its entire mapped contribution is fully subsumed by
   a retained preferred block.
2. Never exclude a partially overlapping block that has a unique contribution
   which could support a new task.
3. Keep at least two blocks.
4. Every candidate block must appear exactly once in retained_block_ids or
   excluded_block_ids.
5. Every excluded block must appear in fully_subsumed_block_ids of an overlap
   group whose preferred block is retained.
6. If uncertain, retain the block and describe partial_overlap.
