Assign one short information_feature to every supplied RIU.

Requirements:
- Use an English noun phrase containing two to six words.
- Describe the broad subject or function of the RIU, not what the RIU concludes.
- Do not include the word "reviewer"; it will be added later.
- Do not include exact amounts, percentages, dates, thresholds, personal names, organization names, locations, answer labels, quoted language, or legal rule, statute, regulation, or section citations.
- Do not state whether a condition is satisfied.
- Do not state that an action is allowed, prohibited, approved, denied, successful, unsuccessful, correct, or incorrect.
- Do not encode positive or negative polarity.
- Do not name a concrete communication method, technology, remedy, or other implementation detail that appears only in the private RIU; generalize it to its broad subject.
- A participant who cannot see the RIU should not be able to reconstruct its private content from the information_feature.
- Use distinct phrases for different subjects when the RIUs support distinct parts of the problem.
- Preserve only established abbreviations that are needed to identify the broad subject.

Examples:
- RIU: "The taxpayer had $75,845 of income." Good: "income amount". Bad: "$75,845 income".
- RIU: "The party knew the information before trading." Good: "party knowledge". Bad: "confirmed party knowledge".
- RIU: "The purchase exceeds the applicable limit." Good: "purchase threshold". Bad: "limit-exceeding purchase".
- RIU: "Alice has single filing status." Good: "filing status". Bad: "single-filer status".
- RIU: "The court ordered technology access." Good: "ordered-relief scope". Bad: "technology-access order".
- RIU: "A motion invokes Rule 12(h)(3)." Good: "jurisdictional motion filing". Bad: "Rule 12(h)(3) filing".
- RIU: "The order requires telephone access." Good: "legal access terms". Bad: "telephone access terms".
