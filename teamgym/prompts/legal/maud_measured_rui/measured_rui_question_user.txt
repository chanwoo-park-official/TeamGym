5. Generate a decision_set of 2 to 6 mutually exclusive candidate final decisions. Prefer the suggested option count when plausible, but do not force it.
6. Every selected RIU must control at least one answer-choice boundary. If that RIU is omitted, the exact oracle answer must change, become indeterminate, or become less specific.

10. Avoid obvious option design. The correct option must not be the only long, conjunctive, two-sided, multi-feature, or most specific option.

12. Use neutral option values decision_a, decision_b, decision_c, decision_d, decision_e, decision_f. Put legal content in decision_option_definitions.
13. If 4-6 RIUs are selected, preserve that complexity: the correct option and at least two distractors should each describe a comparable number of legal components, with different supported/unsupported combinations.

Good pattern:
- If RIU 1 shows a timing period, RIU 2 shows a disclosure condition, and RIU 3 shows a signing/closing condition, ask for the complete trigger profile.
- Wrong options should keep similar complexity but alter one legally plausible component.
- A proper subset that supports a less-complete profile is not sufficient for the exact oracle answer.
- For many-RIU cases, wrong options may alter only one or two components while keeping the rest plausible, so the correct answer cannot be guessed from option length or complexity.
